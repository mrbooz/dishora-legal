What Dishora is

Dishora is a food discovery app. It shows you dishes, and it can invent new ones, rewrite existing ones and reconstruct a recipe from a photograph you choose.

Recipes are generated, and untested

This is the most important thing on this page. Most dishes in Dishora — and every dish you create, remix or generate from a photo — are produced automatically. No person has cooked them before you do.

  • Quantities, timings, temperatures and nutrition figures are estimates. Treat them as a starting point, not an instruction.
  • Cost figures are rough and vary by country, shop and season.
  • Use your own judgement on doneness, especially with meat, poultry, fish, shellfish and eggs. Cook to safe internal temperatures as defined by your local food safety authority, not to the time printed in the app.

Allergies and dietary needs

If you have an allergy or intolerance, read every ingredient yourself and check the packaging of anything you buy. Do not rely on the app for this.
